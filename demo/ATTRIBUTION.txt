Images in this folder (demo/DEMO-MS-03, demo/DEMO-BM-10) are derived from public research data.
They are not patient data from a Lävi study.

  & Battiato, S. (2025). MSLesSeg: baseline and benchmarking of a new Multiple Sclerosis Lesion
  Segmentation dataset [Dataset]. figshare. https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.27919209.v1
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

  Davis, M., Malhotra, A., Bousabarah, K., Deuschl, C., Lin, M., Aneja, S., & Aboian, M. (2025).

  Clark, K., Vendt, B., Smith, K., Freymann, J., Kirby, J., Koppel, P., Moore, S., Phillips, S.,

resampled and converted to WebP. Pixels only; no DICOM text, dates or identifiers.

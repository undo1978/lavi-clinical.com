Lävi Clinical Suite · Longitudinal brain MRI

Not every difference is disease.

Lävi Delta is in development and is not CE-marked.

We develop technology for detecting and assessing meaningful change between serial brain MRI examinations. Lävi Delta, our first product, supports the radiologist in follow-up brain MRI: it shows on a map where the signal has changed between two examinations. When the two cannot be compared on technical grounds, it says so and withholds the result.

01 — What it does

What Lävi Delta does

Lävi Delta compares a person's new brain MRI with their previous one and shows, on a colour map, where the signal has changed. The radiologist checks each coloured area against the original images and decides what it means; the software makes no diagnosis. When two examinations cannot fairly be compared, it says so and shows no result rather than a misleading one.

  • Red: signal increased between the two scans.
  • Blue: signal decreased.
  • No colour: the map shows no change. The stronger the colour, the larger the change.

The map is not a list of lesions and not a diagnosis. The radiologist decides.

02 — Scanners

Same person, two scanners

A follow-up scan is often made on a different scanner from the first one, and some of what looks like change is only the difference between the two machines. Lävi Delta is being built to tell that apart from real change in the patient.

In our internal technical tests, results across scanners from different vendors have been promising. Read what was tested and what was not.

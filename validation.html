Clinical validation & evidence

What we will prove, and how.

No clinical performance claim on this site is made from anything short of independent clinical evidence — and that evidence does not exist yet. This page describes how it will be constructed.

Clinical validation programme in preparation · Status as of 2026-08-20

01 — The question

What a reader study answers

A second-reader tool does not make findings on its own; it changes how a radiologist reads. So the question that matters is not "how accurate is the software?" but "do radiologists read follow-up brain MRI better with it than without it?"

The study design built to answer that question is called a multi-reader, multi-case study — MRMC for short. The same retrospective cases are read by several radiologists, first without the software and later with it, with a deliberate pause between the two rounds so that no reader remembers individual cases. Because every reader reads every case in both conditions, the study can measure whether the software changes reader performance while accounting for differences between readers and differences between cases.

This is the standard of evidence we consider appropriate for a tool of this kind, and it is the standard the site holds itself to: until that study is run and independently analysed, no claim about clinical performance appears here.

03 — The planned study

A retrospective multi-reader, multi-case study

The study in preparation will use one hundred retrospective brain MRI cases across three clinical strata — multiple sclerosis, primary brain tumours, and brain metastases. Multiple radiologists will read every case twice: first unaided, and later aided by Lävi Delta, with an enforced washout period between the two phases.

The reference standard will be established by an independent process, and the statistical analysis will have independent input. Endpoints under preparation cover localisation performance, false-positive burden, reading time and reader confidence, alongside standalone technical performance.

The final statistical design is subject to methodology review. We publish no sample size justification, no power figures, no target effect sizes, and no dates: those belong in the study protocol, not on a website.

04 — The reader environment

The study infrastructure already exists

Reader studies fail on logistics as often as on science, so the reading environment was built first. A dedicated browser-based reader workstation exists and is deployed.

Implemented and tested: role-based access control with two-factor authentication; the blinding gate between arms, so that readers in the unaided phase cannot reach the software's outputs; washout enforcement between phases; structured capture of reader responses; a full audit trail; and a results export for analysis.

The pipeline and this reader environment have been verified to work together end to end against a real deployment — including verifying that cases the pipeline rejects are rejected, not silently passed through.

05 — The boundary

What we have not proven

  1. Clinical performance has not been established. Whether Lävi Delta changes reader performance is precisely what the planned study exists to measure — we do not claim it in advance.

  2. Cross-vendor and cross-protocol comparison is a design goal and an explicit requirement of the validation programme. It is not an established capability, and we do not claim that the software works across scanners or protocols.

  3. Some implemented capabilities — including longitudinal contrast-enhancement change detection — are still under technical evaluation and are not part of any claim.

  4. The absence of a flagged candidate is not evidence that no change occurred. The radiologist reads every study independently; nothing about Lävi Delta reduces that responsibility.

06 — Regulatory pathway

Stages, not dates

Lävi Delta is being developed as a medical device under the EU Medical Device Regulation. The pathway below is stated as stages deliberately without dates: a published timeline is a promise about other parties' decisions, and we do not make promises we do not control.

  1. Product and evidence developmentcurrent stage
  2. Technical verificationcurrent stage
  3. Independent clinical validationin preparation
  4. Quality and technical documentationahead
  5. Conformity assessmentahead
  6. Market deploymentahead

Lävi Delta is under development. It is not CE-marked, is not available for sale, and must not be used for clinical decision-making. Information on this site describes a product in development and its planned evaluation, not the performance of a marketed medical device.
